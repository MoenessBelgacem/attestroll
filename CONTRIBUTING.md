# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. This is an early prototype with a single maintainer.

- **Feedback is the most valuable contribution right now**: open an issue describing how you update
  your devices today and what hurts. No code needed.
- Bug reports with a reproduction are welcome.
- **Pull requests:** please open an issue first. Because the hub is AGPL-3.0 and the author may offer
  a commercial license for it, contributions to AGPL-licensed files cannot be merged until a
  contributor agreement (CLA) process is in place. Contributions to Apache-2.0 files (see `LICENSE`)
  are accepted under the same license, with a `Signed-off-by` line (DCO).
